# Accounting Policies Memo - Portside Logic Pte. Ltd.

_Date: 2026-11-20. DRAFT for review by a Singapore-licensed accountant. Not accounting advice. Policies take effect only when you approve them._

Framework: Singapore financial reporting standards as they apply to a private company of this size. Whether full SFRS or SFRS for Small Entities applies has not been verified (consult the Accounting Standards Committee and ACRA); the policies below are written to work under either and must be confirmed.

1. **Basis and currency.** Accrual basis. Presentation in the functional currency, which is not yet decided (question 8 in the tax checklist). ASSUMPTION for setup only: SGD.
2. **Revenue.** Pricing model is TBD in the profile, so three rules are drafted and the unused ones will be deleted:
   - Subscription: recognised evenly over the service period; amounts billed in advance sit in contract liabilities (2100).
   - Usage (per document or shipment): recognised in the month the documents are processed; unbilled usage accrued to contract assets (1150).
   - Pilot or implementation fees: recognised over the pilot term unless the pilot delivers a separate, completed service.
   - Free pilots: no revenue; costs expensed as cost of revenue.
3. **Cost of revenue.** Production model inference, production cloud, document-processing services, payment fees. Non-production model and cloud usage goes to R&D. Tag cloud projects and API keys "prod" or "dev" so the split comes from the bills, not from estimates.
4. **Development costs.** Expensed as incurred. Capitalisation is permitted by the standards only when strict criteria are met; at pilot stage the future-benefit and cost-measurement tests are hard to evidence and capitalising would add judgement for no cash benefit. Account 1600 stays dormant. Revisit at first audit or fundraise.
5. **Foreign currency.** Transactions at the rate on the transaction date (the accounting system's daily rate). Monetary balances revalued at month-end; differences to 7000/7010. No hedging.
6. **Fixed assets.** Items under S$1,000 expensed (threshold is a proposal); computer equipment depreciated straight-line over 3 years.
7. **Accruals and prepayments.** Model and cloud usage accrued monthly from provider dashboards. Annual plans over S$1,000 prepaid and released monthly.
8. **Expense policy.** Company costs on the company card or account only. Any founder-paid cost is claimed with a receipt within 30 days through 2300. No personal spending through the company. Every transaction has a document attached in the ledger.
9. **Records.** Kept electronically for at least 5 years from the relevant Year of Assessment (IRAS, Record keeping requirements, updated 22 Jan 2026).
10. **Materiality.** Variance threshold is TBD in the profile. PROPOSAL: investigate any account variance above the higher of S$500 or 10% month on month.

Questions for the accountant: which framework applies; whether the revenue rules match the final customer contract; whether the capitalisation and fixed-asset thresholds are acceptable for tax as well as accounts.
