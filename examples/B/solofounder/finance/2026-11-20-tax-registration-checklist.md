## GST trade-off to decide
- For registering: input GST becomes claimable; services to Dutch and German customers may qualify for zero-rating (to confirm); overseas digital-service suppliers generally charge GST to customers that are not GST-registered, so US model and cloud bills may carry unrecoverable 9% GST today (to confirm on your actual invoices).
- Against: quarterly returns, the 2-year lock-in, InvoiceNow from day one, and 9% added to invoices for Singapore customers (recoverable by them only if they are registered).
