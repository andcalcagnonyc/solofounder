On "today", three limits apply:

What can realistically happen today, once you answer the first four questions: your company profile, a shortlist of two or three structures, current formation rules researched from the registry and tax authority with dated sources, a decision memo with my recommendation, and a first draft of the formation documents.

Everything legal and tax I produce is a draft for review by a locally licensed lawyer and tax adviser, not legal advice. I will give you the specific questions to put to them.

### Questions I need answered now (location and legal)

1. **Where will the company's HQ be?** Country, plus state, province, or city where relevant.
2. **Is anything already incorporated?** If not: are you open to incorporating in a different country from the one you live in?
3. **What is your citizenship, and in which country are you tax resident?** Running a foreign company from where you live can create tax consequences there, so the two answers matter separately.
4. **Which countries will your first customers be in, and where are any employees or contractors?**

"Not decided yet" is a valid answer to any of these.

### Next, about the company

5. In one sentence: what does the product do, and for whom?
6. Is it Applied AI (AI embedded in a product or workflow) or Agentic AI (agents completing tasks on their own)? What is the unit of work it performs?
7. What stage are you at: idea, prototype, pilot customers, or revenue?
8. How will you fund it: bootstrapped, angels, venture, or grants? How much, when, and from investors in which countries? Investor expectations often decide the structure.
9. Will any enterprise or public-sector buyer require a local contracting entity?
10. In which countries do you plan to hire in the next 12 months?
11. Are there co-founders or other shareholders, and in what percentages?

### Then, operating constraints

12. Launch target date and hours per week you can give this.
13. Budget for tools and services over the first 90 days, and the currency.
14. Tools you already use (CRM, accounting, payments, code repository, e-signature, cap table).
15. Languages for customer-facing material.
16. Approval preference: approve everything, or only external, financial, and irreversible actions?
