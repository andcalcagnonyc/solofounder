## What I found (with sources and dates)

- **Corporate income tax** is a flat 17%. A new company can exempt 75% of its first S$100,000 and 50% of the next S$100,000 of chargeable income for its first three consecutive Years of Assessment, subject to shareholder conditions. IRAS, CIT rate and exemption schemes, updated 7 Aug 2026.
- **Estimated Chargeable Income** is due within three months of year-end, with a waiver when revenue is S$5 million or below and the estimate is nil. IRAS, ECI filing, updated 10 Aug 2026.
- **Tax return:** the YA 2026 deadline was 30 November 2026; a new company files only after closing its first accounts and starting business or earning income. Form C-S needs revenue of S$5 million or below; C-S (Lite) needs S$200,000 or below. IRAS, filing season 2026, updated 30 Sep 2026.
- **Records** must be kept at least five years from the relevant Year of Assessment; electronic copies are accepted. IRAS, record keeping requirements, updated 22 Jan 2026.
- **GST:** S$1 million compulsory threshold and a two-year minimum stay for voluntary registrants. IRAS, factors to consider before registering voluntarily, updated 30 Jan 2026.
- **InvoiceNow:** companies registering voluntarily within six months of incorporation have been in scope since 1 Nov 2025, and all voluntary applicants since 1 Apr 2026. IRAS, GST InvoiceNow Requirement, updated 8 Oct 2026.
- **ACRA:** AGM within six months of year-end and annual return within seven; a private company can skip the AGM by unanimous resolution or by sending accounts to members within five months. ACRA, AGM and annual return breaches, fetched 20 Nov 2026 (no page date shown).
