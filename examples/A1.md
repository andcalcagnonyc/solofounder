### Facts that change the plan

1. **You live in Italy and may incorporate abroad.** A foreign company managed from Milan can be treated as tax resident in Italy or as having a taxable presence there. This is the main question for the entity decision.
2. **US investors, EU founder and customers.** What the angels expect and what suits your own tax position may differ. Find out what the angels require before choosing.
3. **EU customers and an autonomous agent working on accounting records.** EU privacy and AI rules apply already at the design-partner stage.
4. **Third-party financial data.** The firms' client ledgers need confidentiality and security terms in every customer contract.
5. **Code written by a freelancer in Poland.** The company owns it only if a written assignment says so. Investors will check this.
6. **Budget.** 5,000 EUR for 90 days is tight for formation plus adviser fees. I will ask for quotes before anything is committed, and nothing is spent without your approval.
