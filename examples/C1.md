# Run C1: sales-prospecting-automation (Relay Desk, Inc.)

**Founder request:** "Build me an outbound campaign to 150 e-commerce brands: 80 in the US, 40 in Germany, 30 in Canada."

## Response to the founder

Marcus, the campaign is drafted, but it is not a 150-brand email campaign. **Only the 80 US accounts can be cold-emailed as planned. The 40 German accounts cannot be cold-emailed at all, and the 30 Canadian accounts only contact by contact, where we can prove consent.** Nothing has been sent, loaded, or bought.

I read the company profile (Delaware C-Corporation, revenue stage, USD, ICP of e-commerce brands with 20 to 200 staff, HubSpot / Stripe / GitHub / Datadog / Intercom, none connected to me). There was no decision log, so I created one.

### 1. Country rules (checked 20 November 2026)

This is a draft for review by locally licensed lawyers, not legal advice.

| Country | Requested | Cold email | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 80 | Allowed with conditions | CAN-SPAM covers B2B email. Each email needs accurate headers, an honest subject line, disclosure that it is an ad, a valid postal address, and an opt-out that works for 30 days and is honored within 10 business days. Stated maximum penalty: 53,088 USD per email. Source: [FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), published August 2023, penalty edited January 2024. |
| Germany | 40 | **Excluded** | Section 7(2) no. 2 UWG treats email advertising without prior express consent as an unreasonable nuisance in every case. The only exception is for existing customers. I found no carve-out for business recipients. Source: [section 7 UWG, official text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html), no version date shown on the page. |
| Canada | 30 | Per contact, with proof | CASL requires express or implied consent and puts the burden of proof on us. "Conspicuous publication" works only if the address is published without a no-solicitation statement and the message relates to the person's business role. Every message needs sender identification and an unsubscribe, honored within 10 business days. Source: [CRTC guidance on implied consent](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm), no modification date shown. |

What this means in practice:
- **Germany:** the 40 accounts stay on the list for research only. I am also holding LinkedIn direct messages there until a German lawyer says otherwise (assumption).
- **Canada:** a contact is emailed only if we store the URL where the address is published, the capture date, and a screenshot. Addresses guessed by an enrichment tool do not qualify, so expect fewer than 30.
- **Not verified today:** whether CASL reaches a US sender (I assumed yes), US state laws, and GDPR duties for merely storing German contacts in HubSpot.

Full table, assumptions, and lawyer questions: `solofounder/sales/2026-11-20-outreach-rules-by-country.md`
