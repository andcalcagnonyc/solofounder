# Privacy

solofounder-os is a set of instruction files (skills) for Claude. It contains no code, no servers, no connectors, and no analytics.

- **The plugin collects nothing.** It does not send, receive, or store any data, and its author receives no information about who installs or uses it.
- **Files stay with you.** When you use the skills, Claude writes working files (company profile, decision log, drafts) to the folder you are working in. Those files can contain information about you, your company, your customers, and your prospects. They are stored wherever your Claude session stores files and are under your control.
- **Your conversations with Claude** are handled under the terms and privacy policy of your Claude plan with Anthropic, not by this plugin.
- **Tools you connect** (CRM, accounting, helpdesk, and others) are governed by those providers' own terms. The plugin refers to them by category and ships no connection of its own.
