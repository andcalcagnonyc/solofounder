# Positioning, Messaging, and Product Marketing

Define who the product is for, what job it does, why it wins, and how to say it, then turn that into the messaging source every other marketing and sales skill draws on.

## Before starting

1. Read `solofounder/company-profile.md`. If it does not exist, stop, tell the founder that the `start` intake comes first, name the `start` skill, and run it before anything else.
2. Take HQ country, entity type, stage, currency, and tool stack from the profile. Never assume a jurisdiction, a currency, or a tool.
3. Check `solofounder/decision-log.md` for earlier decisions that constrain this work.

## Inputs

- Product description, evidence from customer conversations, competitors and alternatives
- Target segment hypotheses

## Workflow

1. Define the ideal customer profile and buying committee: firmographics, trigger events, pains in the customer's words, and current alternative (including doing nothing or hiring people).
2. Research the competitive set, including incumbent software, other AI entrants, and in-house builds on foundation models.
3. Write the positioning statement: category, target, problem, outcome delivered, proof, and differentiator. For agentic products, state the unit of work the agent completes and the outcome sold.
4. Build the messaging hierarchy: one-line promise, three pillars each with proof points, objection responses, and the trust message (accuracy, oversight, data handling).
5. Write persona-specific versions for economic buyer, user, and technical evaluator.
6. Produce launch assets list and the product-marketing plan for each release: audience, message, channels, enablement.
7. Create competitive battlecards with honest strengths and weaknesses.
8. Define how the messaging will be tested (landing-page variants, outbound reply rates, sales-call reactions) and when it is revisited.

## Deliverables

- ICP and persona document
- Positioning statement and messaging hierarchy
- Battlecards
- Launch plan template

## Founder approval gates

- Target segment choice
- Final positioning and any comparative claim about a competitor

## Guardrails

- Act through connected tools where they exist (see `CONNECTORS.md`); otherwise produce ready-to-use files and exact manual steps.
- Ask before any action that contacts someone outside the company, spends money, or cannot be undone.
- Mark assumptions as assumptions. State a fact about the company only when it is in the profile or the founder said it in this session; otherwise ask. Never invent facts, metrics, customers, quotes, or citations.
- Append each material decision to `solofounder/decision-log.md` with date, options considered, and rationale.
- Save deliverables under `solofounder/marketing/` with dated filenames.

## Hand-offs

- `marketing-website` and `marketing-brand-and-design` for execution
- `sales-icp-and-playbook` for sales use
- `product-strategy-and-roadmap` for feedback into product
