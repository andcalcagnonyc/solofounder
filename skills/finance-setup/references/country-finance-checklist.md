# Country Finance Checklist

Use this list to research the finance and tax requirements for the company's legal form and country. Answer every item from the tax authority, the company registry, or the accounting standard-setter, cite the source with its date, and have a local accountant confirm.

## Accounting and records
- Which accounting framework applies to this legal form and size (local GAAP, IFRS, simplified regime)?
- Is there a mandatory chart of accounts or statutory layout?
- Functional and reporting currency rules
- Bookkeeping language and location requirements
- Record-retention period and acceptable digital formats
- Audit thresholds and whether a statutory auditor is required
- Annual accounts: content, approval body, filing deadline, publication

## Invoicing
- Mandatory invoice content and numbering rules
- Electronic-invoicing mandate and the required channel or format
- Rules for credit notes and corrections
- Fiscal-device or real-time reporting requirements

## Indirect tax (VAT, GST, sales tax)
- Registration threshold and voluntary registration
- Rates that apply to software and digital services
- Place-of-supply rules for cross-border business and consumer sales
- Reverse-charge rules and one-stop or simplified schemes
- Filing frequency and payment deadlines
- For the United States: state-by-state economic nexus and taxability of software

## Corporate income tax
- Rate, instalments, return deadline
- Loss carry-forward rules
- Deductibility and capitalisation of development costs
- R&D incentives, innovation regimes, startup reliefs
- Withholding taxes on cross-border payments
- Transfer-pricing documentation thresholds

## People
- Payroll registration, employer contributions, filing cycle
- Founder remuneration: salary versus dividend rules
- Contractor reporting obligations
- Equity-compensation tax treatment and approved schemes

## Banking and payments
- Documents needed to open a business account for this legal form
- Capital-deposit requirements
- Payment processors that support the country and legal form
- Foreign-exchange or capital controls

## Calendar
- Financial year-end rules
- All recurring deadlines entered into the compliance calendar
