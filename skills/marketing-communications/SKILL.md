# Communications, PR, and Social

Build awareness and credibility through the founder's own voice, earned media, and launch moments, on a cadence a single person can sustain.

## Before starting

1. Read `solofounder/company-profile.md`. If it does not exist, stop, tell the founder that the `start` intake comes first, name the `start` skill, and run it before anything else.
2. Take HQ country, entity type, stage, currency, and tool stack from the profile. Never assume a jurisdiction, a currency, or a tool.
3. Check `solofounder/decision-log.md` for earlier decisions that constrain this work.

## Inputs

- Messaging, founder story, proof points, launch dates
- Founder's writing samples for voice

## Workflow

1. Define the narrative: the change in the market, the founder's point of view, and what the company proves about it.
2. Build the editorial calendar: founder posts, product updates, customer stories, opinion pieces, and newsletter, mapped to channels.
3. Draft content in the founder's voice from their samples; vary formats and keep each piece to one idea.
4. Plan each launch: goal, date, assets, channel sequence, community seeding, and a run-of-show for launch day.
5. Build the media and influencer list for the category; draft tailored pitches and a press kit (boilerplate, founder bio, images, facts).
6. Write announcements for funding, product, partnership, and milestones.
7. Prepare a holding statement and response plan for incidents, including AI errors that reach customers.
8. Report monthly: reach, engagement, mentions, and inbound attributed to communications.

## Deliverables

- Narrative and editorial calendar
- Post, newsletter, and announcement drafts
- Press kit and media list
- Launch run-of-show
- Crisis holding statements

## Founder approval gates

- Every post, pitch, and announcement before it goes out
- Any statement about customers, investors, or partners
- Any incident statement

## Guardrails

- Act through connected tools where they exist (see `CONNECTORS.md`); otherwise produce ready-to-use files and exact manual steps.
- Ask before any action that contacts someone outside the company, spends money, or cannot be undone.
- Mark assumptions as assumptions. State a fact about the company only when it is in the profile or the founder said it in this session; otherwise ask. Never invent facts, metrics, customers, quotes, or citations.
- Append each material decision to `solofounder/decision-log.md` with date, options considered, and rationale.
- Save deliverables under `solofounder/marketing/` with dated filenames.

## Hand-offs

- `marketing-demand-generation` for campaign alignment
- `ops-incident-response` for incident communications
