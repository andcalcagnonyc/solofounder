# Field Marketing, Events, Community, and Partners

Put the founder in front of buyers and allies in person and through partners, with each activity tied to named accounts and followed up within 48 hours.

## Before starting

1. Read `solofounder/company-profile.md`. If it does not exist, stop, tell the founder that the `start` intake comes first, name the `start` skill, and run it before anything else.
2. Take HQ country, entity type, stage, currency, and tool stack from the profile. Never assume a jurisdiction, a currency, or a tool.
3. Check `solofounder/decision-log.md` for earlier decisions that constrain this work.

## Inputs

- ICP and target-account list, geography, budget, calendar

## Workflow

1. Select events by concentration of target accounts, cost, and speaking or demo access. Rank them.
2. For each event, set the plan: target meetings booked in advance, talk or demo, materials, and capture method.
3. Design owned formats that suit one person: small dinners, virtual roundtables, live build sessions, office hours.
4. Run pre-event outreach to target accounts and post-event follow-up sequences with notes logged in the CRM.
5. Build community presence: the forums, groups, and open-source projects where the buyers and builders are; contribute before promoting.
6. Define the partner motion: integration partners, model and cloud marketplaces, agencies and consultancies, referral terms, and co-marketing.
7. Measure each activity: meetings, opportunities, and cost per opportunity.

## Deliverables

- Ranked event and field plan with budget
- Event playbooks and follow-up sequences
- Community plan
- Partner program outline and target list

## Founder approval gates

- Event spend and travel
- Partner terms and any revenue share
- Outreach to partners and attendees

## Guardrails

- Act through connected tools where they exist (see `CONNECTORS.md`); otherwise produce ready-to-use files and exact manual steps.
- Ask before any action that contacts someone outside the company, spends money, or cannot be undone.
- Mark assumptions as assumptions. State a fact about the company only when it is in the profile or the founder said it in this session; otherwise ask. Never invent facts, metrics, customers, quotes, or citations.
- Append each material decision to `solofounder/decision-log.md` with date, options considered, and rationale.
- Save deliverables under `solofounder/marketing/` with dated filenames.

## Hand-offs

- `sales-prospecting-automation` for follow-up
- `legal-commercial-contracts` for partner agreements
