# Website and Landing Pages

Ship a website that explains the product, earns trust, and converts visitors into trials, demos, or waitlist sign-ups, with analytics in place from the first visit.

## Before starting

1. Read `solofounder/company-profile.md`. If it does not exist, stop, tell the founder that the `start` intake comes first, name the `start` skill, and run it before anything else.
2. Take HQ country, entity type, stage, currency, and tool stack from the profile. Never assume a jurisdiction, a currency, or a tool.
3. Check `solofounder/decision-log.md` for earlier decisions that constrain this work.

## Inputs

- Messaging hierarchy, brand system, pricing, proof (demos, results, logos, security posture)
- Conversion goal: self-serve sign-up, demo request, or waitlist

## Workflow

1. Define the site map for launch: home, product or use cases, pricing, security and trust, about, blog or resources, legal pages, and conversion page.
2. Write each page problem-first: headline promise, how it works, proof, objections, single primary call to action.
3. Show the product working: demo video, interactive example, or sample output, with honest limits stated.
4. Build the site on the stack in the profile through ~~CMS or code; make it responsive, accessible, and fast.
5. Implement technical foundations: metadata, structured data, sitemap, robots and AI-crawler policy, canonical URLs, analytics, consent management matched to the privacy rules of visitor locations.
6. Wire conversion paths end to end: form or checkout to CRM record, confirmation email, calendar booking, and alert to the founder.
7. Test before launch: links, forms, mobile, speed, accessibility, legal pages present.
8. After launch, review conversion weekly and run one experiment at a time.

## Deliverables

- Site map and page copy
- Built site or build-ready specifications
- Launch checklist results
- Conversion tracking plan

## Founder approval gates

- Final copy and every factual or performance claim
- Publishing to the production domain
- Use of any customer name or logo

## Guardrails

- Act through connected tools where they exist (see `CONNECTORS.md`); otherwise produce ready-to-use files and exact manual steps.
- Ask before any action that contacts someone outside the company, spends money, or cannot be undone.
- Mark assumptions as assumptions. State a fact about the company only when it is in the profile or the founder said it in this session; otherwise ask. Never invent facts, metrics, customers, quotes, or citations.
- Append each material decision to `solofounder/decision-log.md` with date, options considered, and rationale.
- Save deliverables under `solofounder/marketing/` with dated filenames.
