# 14-Day Launch Plan

Reference plan. Tailor it to the company profile before use. Long-lead third-party steps (registration, banking, tax IDs) start on day 1 and complete whenever the jurisdiction allows.

| Day | Goal | Skills | Key deliverables |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Profile, plan, long-lead items started | `start`, `launch-sprint`, `legal-entity-formation`, `product-discovery-and-validation` | Company profile, sprint plan, entity decision memo, hypotheses and interview guide |
| 2 | Customer evidence; tool stack | `product-discovery-and-validation`, `stack-and-automation-setup`, `marketing-positioning-and-messaging` | Interviews running, stack decision, draft ICP and positioning |
| 3 | **G1 Problem.** Scope decided | `product-strategy-and-roadmap`, `product-pricing-and-packaging` | Decision memo, MVP scope, pricing hypothesis, design-partner commitment |
| 4 | Specification and architecture; formation filed | `product-requirements-and-specs`, `product-ux-design`, `eng-architecture`, `legal-founder-equity-and-cap-table` | PRD, agent spec, evaluation cases, architecture, founder share and IP documents |
| 5 | **G2 Foundation.** Repo, environments, finance stack | `eng-build-workflow`, `ops-infrastructure-and-deploy`, `finance-setup`, `marketing-brand-and-design` | Repo with CI, staging environment, chart of accounts, brand basics |
| 6 | Build core workflow | `eng-agent-design`, `eng-data-and-retrieval`, `eng-evals` | Agent v1, data pipeline, baseline evaluation |
| 7 | Build; contracts and privacy | `eng-build-workflow`, `legal-commercial-contracts`, `legal-privacy-and-ai-compliance` | Working end-to-end path, contract templates, privacy notice and data map |
| 8 | Build; website | `eng-build-workflow`, `eng-qa-testing`, `marketing-website`, `marketing-seo-geo` | Feature-complete MVP, test suite, website draft, topic map |
| 9 | Harden | `eng-security-and-ai-safety`, `eng-evals`, `ops-observability`, `ops-security-and-reliability` | Threat model and fixes, evaluation pass, dashboards and alerts, backup and restore test |
| 10 | **G3 Product.** Release to production | `eng-code-review-and-release`, `ops-incident-response`, `ops-cost-and-finops` | Production release, runbooks, cost per task |
| 11 | Sales engine | `sales-icp-and-playbook`, `sales-crm-and-funnel`, `sales-prospecting-automation` | Playbook, CRM configured, first sequence approved |
| 12 | **G4 Go-to-market.** Site live, demand programs | `marketing-website`, `marketing-demand-generation`, `marketing-communications`, `sales-qualification-and-discovery` | Live site, launch campaign, announcement drafts, meetings booked |
| 13 | Support and billing | `support-setup`, `support-knowledge-base`, `support-ai-agent`, `finance-invoicing-and-collections` | Helpdesk, help articles, support agent, billing configured |
| 14 | **G5 Launch.** Onboard pilots; start routines | `support-customer-success`, `support-ticket-triage-and-response`, `sales-proposal-and-close`, `finance-bookkeeping-and-close`, `legal-compliance-calendar`, `founder-weekly-review` | Pilots onboarded, first invoices, books current, compliance calendar, weekly review scheduled |

## After day 14

| Horizon | Focus | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 15-30 | Convert pilots, first month-end close, governance | `sales-pipeline-review-and-forecast`, `finance-bookkeeping-and-close`, `legal-corporate-governance`, `product-analytics-and-feedback` |
| Days 31-60 | Repeatable demand, payables and payroll, IP | `marketing-field-and-partners`, `finance-payables-and-payroll`, `legal-ip-and-trademarks`, `legal-hiring-and-contractors` |
| Days 61-90 | Tax period, runway, raise decision | `finance-tax-and-statutory-compliance`, `finance-planning-and-runway`, `finance-fundraising-and-investor-relations`, `legal-fundraising` |
